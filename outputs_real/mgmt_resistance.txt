What this cannot show:
  - that MGMT methylation changes any patient's outcome: it is a prognostic marker, and measuring its association in a cohort is not a mechanism
  - that the mapped resistance is the real resistance: the mapping is a declared convention anchored on the calibrated default, not a fit
  - that the simulated PFS differences are treatment effects: the engine's dynamics class is anchored to a prostate adaptive-therapy trial, and no GBM outcome is reproduced here
  - that a beta from a donor tumour equals the patient's own: the panel is a cohort distribution, and the engine consumes one beta per simulated case
